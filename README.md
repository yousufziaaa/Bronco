# Bronco

## Overview
Bronco was a platform designed to support American industry in a number of ways. During the 8 months I worked as their lead designer, they oscillated between different product offerings as they attempted to find a product market fit. This resulted in numerous designs and design languages being developed, only to ultimately be scrapped. However, a few weeks into the process, I was able to develop a unified design system to create a cohesive feel across all of their different products.

## Features
* ERP for inventory management
* Worfklow builder
* Automated email negotiations
* Live price tracking and vendor bidding
* Real time alerts by AI

## Technologies Used
* Figma
* Framer

## Challenges
- **Problem:** The biggest challenge that was frequently faces was the turnaround time. Many design requests would come in and need to be addressed within short time frames, often between 4-24 hours. This resulted in a lot of the user testing and scoping out the problem work to be glossed over, as the main task ws ultimately to design screens that the developers could ship.
- **Solution:** Working on a remote team and being on a contract, it was often difficult to fully understand the product or feature I was being asked to develop. To combat this, I began to use task-handoff meetings to focus on asking clarifying questions and fully understanding the feature I was responsible for designing. 

## Learnings
- The biggest learning from my work with Bronco was that when approaching any design task, a deep understanding of what you're building and how it relates to existing or planned features is crucial. Having deep systems level knowledge of all the interconnected parts would make it a lot easier to design cohesive, seamless products and experiences. 

## Future Work
- If I were still working on the same projects with Bronco, I would have focused more on making features easier to access. Due to the fact that most ERPs are extremely complex from a user perspective, most of the design inspiration came from these products and influenced the designs I also created. Going back, I would've spent longer focusing on how the user experience could be simplified to create beautiful, seamless product experiences.
